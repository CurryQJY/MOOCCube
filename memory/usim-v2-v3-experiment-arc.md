Iterative repair experiments run 2026-07-22 to fix the CKG-RL train/inference mismatch (see [[ckg-rl-cold-start-diagnosis]]). All single-seed 2025, same strict split (hash identical across V2/V3/V3.1, 68 cold / 575 hot items), item-macro @10 unless noted.

**Baselines for comparison:**
- 旧版 2025 (course_ablation_e60_3seed/full, seed 2025): Cold R/N=.2532/.1835, Hot R/N=.2181/.1342, Overall R/N=.2218/.1394. Strong hot/overall.
- 旧版 2667 (three-seed mean, same run): Cold R/N=.2667/.1962. This is the paper's headline cold number.
- `.2863` = paper CKG-RL three-seed cold course-macro R@10 mean; its matching seed-2025 raw is `.2732`. NOT directly comparable to single-seed V2/V3 — user often conflates these.

**V2 (usim_original_v2)** — pseudo-cold + frozen warm teacher, rollout h_T enters ranking loss, transition h_{t+1}=h_t+0.05·e_u, no learned a_end (fixed 5 steps), single-positive MC recommendation reward. Result: Cold R/N=.2849/.1869 (strongest cold), but Hot R/N=.1408/.0802, Overall=.1560/.0915 — hot/overall collapse. Early-stopped epoch 7, restored epoch 1. Four blockers fixed during build: teacher-checkpoint compat loading (added strict shape/key validation), PPO actor-gradient cancellation (old_log_probs not detached), old-actor contamination (fresh V3 actor required), batch-row-index misused as item-ID → CUDA OOB.

**V3 (ckg_rl_usim_v3)** — full USIM engine: explicit a_end, replay RecPPO, terminal value anchor, all-positive recommendation reward, chunked user retrieval, CKG candidate bias. Result WORSE: Cold R/N=.2367/.1604. Root cause NOT a_end: two integration bugs — (1) candidate set filled 20 slots with residual_top first, starving positive/state/random quotas → train sees Top20(e_i-h_t), inference sees Top20(h_t), only 52% candidate overlap; (2) CKG sampler only reranked same candidates → no real knowledge injection into policy (course reward −0.0044 vs rec reward +0.852).

**V3.1** — fixed candidate quota (30/30/30/10), CKG fit added directly to actor logits (beta=0.2) + into PPO replay, audit logging, bounded random sampling. Result: Cold R/N=.2542/.1815, Hot=.1464/.0841, Overall=.1578/.0944. Pulled V3's cold back up, slightly beat V2 overall, but STILL fails vs 旧版 2025 (cold ~parity, hot/overall collapsed). ~2.4× slower than V3 (1120s vs 466s/epoch) due to CKG-fit cost on diversified candidates. Best checkpoint epoch 1; later epochs overfit/drift. Proved simulator is NOT dead but did not solve hot-retention.

**Key lesson (V2/V3/V3.1):** the cold-vs-hot tradeoff is the central unsolved problem. Pushing cold via pseudo-cold/frozen-teacher training collapses hot because shared content_proj/gate still drift under cold loss. Do not attribute gains to any single fix without controlled ablations (isolate CKG logit bias vs candidate quota). This motivated the clean rebuild in [[v32-clean-three-stage-design]].

## Continuation: V3.2-clean through V3.6 (also 2026-07-22 to 07-23)

The clean three-stage rebuild ([[v32-clean-three-stage-design]]) was actually implemented and run, followed by four more isolated iterations. All single-seed 2025 unless noted, each in its own isolated script/output root, TDD throughout,旧路线/结果保留不动.

**V3.2-clean (ckg_rl_usim_v32_clean)** — implemented full T→G→policy pipeline (teacher 20ep, G 30ep, policy 15ep), deterministic H_G/P_train/P_val split, test-after-selection guard, no warm checkpoint, no random mask. First run picked policy epoch 0 — but that "epoch 0" was a random-actor rollout, NOT a true no-sim baseline (bug: `collect_validation(0)` still called `engine.rollout()`). Fixed so epoch 0 = pure `G(c_i)` identity (`seed2025_identity` root). Corrected result: chose PPO epoch 15. Test Cold R/N=.24952/.13882, Hot=.22773/.15172, Overall=.23003/.15036. On C_val, PPO beat legitimate identity by cold N@10 +0.01075 → PPO is NOT a useless component. But cold/overall both below main-table best baselines (CGRC cold .2589/.1845, SEMCo overall R .2502) — cannot claim SOTA, cannot replace paper's `.2863/.2098` row.

**旧强路线审计 (legacy .2863/.2098):** exactly reproduced from recovered source across three seeds (hash-identical) — NOT a fluke. No train/test leak, no warm-checkpoint contamination. BUT `.2863/.2098` is from a legacy evaluator with an unfixable semantic flaw: full-ranking generates the cold vector once for the whole catalog bank, then generates a *separate random vector* for the positive sample and overwrites its score — same cold course has two representations in one ranking. Also train/inference mismatch (sim target = hot item's real ID vector in training, absent at cold inference). Verdict: reusable = strict split, train-only history, catalog/concept graph, base encoders; must discard = legacy positive-recompute eval, random-candidate eval, legacy_id anchor, and writing `.2863` back to the main table. Its overall is very weak (.1568/.0879) — only a "cold expert", never an overall model. Semantic-repair seed-2025 cold = .3062/.2068, overall = .1454/.0817.

**V3.3 (rank_distill)** — replaced generator's vector MSE with panel-KL rank distillation over a 48-user panel. FAILED: cold collapsed to .0798/.0407. Root cause = the rank generator, not PPO. It lowered local 48-user panel KL (.794→.602) but wrecked global geometry (full-199k-user KL .190→.258, mean cosine to teacher .234→.100). Cold was dead *before* rollout. Lesson: local panel KL is a bad global-representation surrogate.

**V3.4 (rank_reward_control)** — single-variable causal control: froze V3.2's vector-only generator (hash-identical), only swapped PPO reward to V3.3's rank gain. All 15 PPO epochs had negative P_val rank gain → correctly fell back to identity (= V3.2 replay exactly). Diagnostic: 89.5% of P_train pseudo-cold items HAVE a positive one-step rank-gain action in their 20 legal candidates, but random-candidate mean gain is negative and PPO never learned to pick them. So: rank-reward PPO fails on optimization/credit-assignment, not empty action space.

**V3.5 (action_distill)** — counterfactual action distillation: enumerate 20 legal candidate actions per P_train state, use teacher one-step rank gain as soft action label (`softmax(gain/0.005)`), distill actor on its own greedy-rollout states. First POSITIVE P_val gain (+0.004718, chose epoch 15) where V3.4 PPO was all-negative — proves the legal action space is learnable. BUT only ~7% of the greedy oracle ceiling (+0.06744), action agreement only 0.239. Teacher/generator hashes identical to V3.4/V3.2. Frozen-checkpoint test replay (diagnostic only — seed-2025 test already seen): Cold .25009/.13747 (+.01358/+.00612 vs identity), Hot .22828/.15177 (−.00138/−.00243), Overall .23059/.15025 (+.00020/−.00152). Cold improved but hot/overall NDCG slipped.

**Pareto framing (V3.5 vs 旧强):** neither dominates. 旧强 cold .3062/.2068 but overall only .1454/.0817; V3.5 cold .2501/.1375 but overall .2306/.1503 (+.0852/+.0686 R/N). Core conflict = 旧路线 pushes cold vectors too aggressively → hot ranking collapse; V3.5 restored overall but cold gain insufficient.

**V3.6 (global_stable_distill) — IMPLEMENTED, NOT YET RUN.** Two ablatable switches: (1) expert-trajectory mixture (50/50 expert-argmax vs actor states, teacher only on P_train) to fix the 0.239 action-agreement / 7%-of-oracle problem; (2) global stability penalty — penalize teacher-score drift on 128 fixed H_G/H_train anchor users so cold vectors don't interfere with hot ranking (calibrated λ=10: cuts global drift 3.22e-4→1.45e-4 at ~4% local-gain cost; λ=30 too strong, inflates END). P_val-only preregistered gate (target ~0.02 = ~30% of oracle). 10/10 unit+tiny-pipeline tests pass, syntax/diff-check pass, NO outputs/checkpoints created. **User halted before the formal seed-2025 run** because a separate time-efficiency experiment is occupying GPU/CPU — must not compete for resources. Files: `ckg_rl_usim_v36_global_stable_distill.py`, `tests/test_ckg_rl_usim_v36_global_stable_distill.py`, `run_ckg_rl_usim_v36_global_stable_distill_seed2025.ps1`.

**Overall verdict of the whole arc:** USIM/simulator is NOT proven dead but has NOT reached publishable main-method strength. The legacy `.2863/.2098` cannot be written back to the main table (unfixable eval semantics). No version simultaneously wins cold AND overall. Next planned step is running V3.6 in a later window, then — only if P_val gain approaches oracle — a fresh unseen strict-cold split/seed (seed-2025 test is now contaminated by repeated exploration and can only give diagnostic numbers). Paper narrative should be "counterfactual action distillation with global ranking-stability constraint", not "simulating real user interaction".
