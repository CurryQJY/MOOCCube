\section{Computational Cost Details}

We compare CKG-RL with CGRC, the graph-reconstruction baseline closest to the
main experimental setting. Let \(E=|\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{train}}|\), \(I=|\mathcal{C}|\), and
\(Q=|\mathcal{D}_{eval}|\). Let \(d\) be the embedding size, \(L\) the number of
graph propagation layers, \(R\) the reconstructed-edge count, \(n_{-}\) the
sampled ranking negatives in CGRC, \(H\) the simulator hidden width, \(T\) the
simulator horizon, \(M\) the retrieved-user count per simulator step, and \(N\)
the sampled-candidate count per simulator step. Table~\ref{tab:supp-complexity}
uses the same full-catalog scoring convention for both methods.

\begin{table*}[t]
\centering

\label{tab:supp-complexity}
\tablecaptiongap
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4.0pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.10}
\begin{tabular}{lp{5.5cm}p{4.6cm}p{4.7cm}}
\toprule
Method & Training cost per epoch & Full-ranking inference cost & Main extra cost \\
\midrule
CGRC & \(O(LEd + Rd + E n_{-}d)\) & \(O(LEd + Rd + QId)\) & Cold-course graph reconstruction and graph propagation. \\
CKG-RL & \(O(E(d + T(Md + Nd + H)))\) plus auxiliary content and prerequisite losses & \(O(IH + QId)\), or \(O(QId)\) after course vectors are cached & Retrieval-based simulator with fixed \(T=5\), \(M=256\), and \(N=20\). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

This comparison separates training overhead from final ranking overhead. CKG-RL
adds a short retrieval-based simulation for each positive instance during
training, but the added factors are fixed hyperparameters rather than catalog
size. CGRC instead incurs graph reconstruction and graph propagation costs. At
test time, CKG-RL does not roll out the simulator for every candidate course; once
course vectors are available, the dominant full-ranking term is the same
\(O(QId)\) dot-product scan used by embedding methods.

Table~\ref{tab:supp-runtime} reports the timing evidence that can be separated
from experiment logs and eval-only profiling. The reported CKG-RL epoch values
are training-loop timers before validation ranking, so they exclude validation
and test evaluation. The inference profiler reloads a retained checkpoint, warms
up the evaluator, and times final cold+hot full-ranking course-macro inference;
data loading, checkpoint loading, and metric-file writing are excluded. CKG-RL
inference includes course-bank construction, while CGRC inference includes
test-time graph reconstruction and graph propagation. Missing entries are left
unavailable rather than estimated from accuracy logs. The MOOCCube CKG-RL
training timer comes from the main-result logs; for CKG-RL latency only, we
profile retained checkpoints from the Full-family runs on the original main
splits with the same inference graph.

These timings are training-loop and eval-only measurements rather than
end-to-end wall-clock runtimes. On COCO, where both methods expose separable
epoch timers, CKG-RL is faster per logged training epoch than CGRC. For retained
CKG-RL checkpoints on MOOCCube, Junyi, and COCO, final full-ranking course-macro
inference is dominated by the cached full-catalog dot-product scan. Where CGRC
checkpoints are available, its inference additionally reconstructs cold-course
edges and propagates the reconstructed graph. The table therefore supports an
inference comparison on Junyi and COCO, and a training-loop comparison on COCO;
other CGRC cost entries are deliberately reported as unavailable.
